# Rates and Limits

> US federal and state contribution limits, tax brackets, deductions and wage
> rates - every figure quoted from the government document it was read from,
> and dated with the day it was last checked against that document.

Each page below carries the sentence its figure was read from, the byte offset
of that sentence in the source document, a stored snapshot of the document as
it was fetched, and the date of the last verification. Where a figure was
corrected, the correction and what it replaced are both published.

The same data is available as a free JSON API with no key and no rate limit,
documented at https://ratesandlimits.com/api. One request to
https://ratesandlimits.com/api/v1/latest.json returns every program's current
figures - each with its quote, byte offset, snapshot and verification date -
rather than one request per page below.

## The site itself

- [Rates and Limits](https://ratesandlimits.com/): Every program this site publishes, grouped by subject.
- [What changed](https://ratesandlimits.com/changes): Figures that moved recently, newest first, each against the year before it.
- [About](https://ratesandlimits.com/about): Who publishes this site and what it does and does not claim.
- [How these figures are verified](https://ratesandlimits.com/methodology): How a figure gets from a government document to a page: the quote, the byte offset, the snapshot and the verification date.
- [Corrections](https://ratesandlimits.com/corrections): Every correction made to a published figure, and what it was corrected from.
- [Calculators](https://ratesandlimits.com/calculators): Every tool on the site, each computing with one year of published figures.
- [Search](https://ratesandlimits.com/search): Search every published figure by program, year or jurisdiction.
- [Glossary](https://ratesandlimits.com/glossary): The terms these figures are stated in, defined once.
- [Sources](https://ratesandlimits.com/sources): Every government document cited, with the snapshot taken of it.
- [API and embeds](https://ratesandlimits.com/api): The free JSON API and the embeddable figure cards, both keyless.
- [Contact](https://ratesandlimits.com/contact): How to report a wrong figure or a dead source.
- [Privacy](https://ratesandlimits.com/privacy): What this site collects, which is close to nothing.
- [Terms of use](https://ratesandlimits.com/terms): The terms these figures are published under.
